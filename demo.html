    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Answers to the questions our clients ask the most.

    How long does a typical project take?

    Most engagements ship a v1 in 4–8 weeks. Complex platforms (ERP/AI) typically run 3–6 months in agile sprints with weekly demos so you see progress every week.

    Do you sign NDAs before sharing project details?

    Yes. We sign mutual NDAs at the very first contact — your idea stays 100% confidential. We can also work under your custom legal framework.

    Who owns the source code after the project?

    You do. 100% IP transfer is included in every contract. Code is delivered via your private GitHub/GitLab with full commit history.

    What if I'm not happy with the work?

    We offer a 7-day risk-free trial on dedicated team engagements and unlimited revisions during fixed-price projects. Plus a 60-day post-launch warranty on every release.

    Do you work across time zones?

    Absolutely. With offices in India, Dubai, Uganda & Australia, we have at least 4-hour overlap with any client time zone — and a dedicated PM available 24/7 for critical issues.

    What technologies do you specialize in?

    Web: React, Vue, Next.js, Laravel, Node, Python · Mobile: Flutter, React Native, Swift, Kotlin · AI: OpenAI, LangChain, PyTorch · Cloud: AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes. Full stack list on the Technology section above.

    How do payments & invoicing work?

    Fixed-price: 30% upfront, 40% mid-milestone, 30% on delivery. Dedicated team: monthly invoicing in USD/EUR/INR. We accept wire transfer, Stripe and PayPal — and never charge until you approve the SOW.
