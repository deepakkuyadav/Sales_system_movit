The Challenge

The client ran 6 D2C brands on 4 different platforms (Shopify, Magento, custom). Each brand had its own tech debt; consolidated reporting was impossible. They needed a single headless layer they could own — without forcing every brand into one frontend.

Our Approach

We built OmniCart — a unified commerce backbone with brand-specific storefronts:

  • Headless commerce core — Medusa-based, customer/order/inventory unified across brands.
  • 6 Next.js storefronts — shared design system, brand-specific themes, edge-rendered.
  • One-page checkout — Apple Pay / Google Pay / Stripe Link, address autocomplete, 0.8s P95.
  • Unified CDP — single customer view across brands, cross-brand recommendations.
  • Migration toolkit — moved 1.4M orders & 2.8M customers with zero downtime.
