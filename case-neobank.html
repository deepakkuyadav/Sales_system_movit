The Challenge

The client had three legacy onboarding flows acquired through M&A. Time-to-first-deposit averaged 9 days, drop-off was 62%, and each new country required 6 months of compliance work. They needed a single platform that could:

  • Onboard a customer in under 5 minutes with selfie + document KYC
  • Comply with eIDAS, AMLD6, GDPR and 12 country-specific regulations
  • Handle multi-currency wallets with real-time FX
  • Scale to 5M+ customers within 18 months

Our Approach

We rebuilt onboarding as an event-driven micro-frontend with regional compliance plugins. Key engineering bets:

  • Modular KYC engine — pluggable providers (Onfido, Veriff, Sumsub) selected per country and risk score.
  • Liveness + ML fraud scoring — sub-2-second decisions, >99% TPR at 0.1% FPR.
  • Multi-currency ledger — double-entry, immutable audit log, 7 base currencies, 28 supported.
  • Compliance-as-code — country rules in declarative YAML, expanded from 3 to 12 countries in 5 months.
  • Mobile-first — Flutter app with offline document capture & resumable uploads.
