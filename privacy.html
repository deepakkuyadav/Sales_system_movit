Privacy Policy

Your trust is the foundation of our business. Here's exactly how we handle your data.

1. Introduction

TechnoBren Infotech Private Limited ("TechnoBren", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect from visitors to technobren.com and clients of our services, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact details — name, email, phone, company, country (when you fill our contact form, request a quote or subscribe to our newsletter).
  • Project information — service interest, budget, timeline and any details you share in messages or attachments.
  • Account information — for clients using our portals (project credentials, billing).

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, device type, browser, operating system.
  • Pages visited, referring URL, time spent, click events.
  • Approximate location (city / country) inferred from IP.

3. How we use your data

  • To respond to enquiries and deliver the services you request.
  • To prepare proposals, contracts and invoices.
  • To improve our website, services and marketing relevance.
  • To send service-related notices and (with consent) newsletters.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.

We rely on the following legal bases under GDPR: performance of a contract, legitimate interest, consent, and legal obligation.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Sub-processors we use to deliver services (cloud hosting, email, analytics, CRM). All sign DPAs with us.
  • Authorities if required by law or court order.
  • Successors in the event of a merger or acquisition (we will notify you).

5. Data security

We follow industry-standard security practices including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based access control, mandatory MFA for staff, regular penetration testing, and ISO 27001-aligned controls. No system is 100% secure — we will notify affected users within 72 hours of confirmed breach.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR for EU/UK residents, CCPA for California, DPDP Act 2023 for India) you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email privacy@technobren.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & tracking

We use essential cookies (session, security) and, with consent, analytics cookies (Google Analytics). See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

8. Data retention

  • Enquiry data — 24 months from last contact.
  • Client/project data — 7 years (statutory tax requirement).
  • Marketing list — until you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs — 90 days.

9. International transfers

Your data may be processed in India, the UAE and the EU where our offices and sub-processors are located. We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission for transfers outside the EEA.

10. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be communicated by email or website banner.
