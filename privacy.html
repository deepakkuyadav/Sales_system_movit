Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026 · Effective immediately for all visitors and clients.

TechnoBren Infotech Private Limited ("TechnoBren", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact data: Name, work email, phone, company — submitted via forms.
  • Project data: Project briefs, requirements and any files you share with us.
  • Usage data: Anonymized analytics (page views, clicks) via privacy-friendly tools.
  • Cookies: Strictly-necessary & analytics cookies (you can disable in browser settings).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your inquiry, prepare proposals, and deliver contracted services.
  • To send transactional emails (quotes, invoices, project updates).
  • With your explicit consent, to send marketing emails — unsubscribe anytime.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud.

3. Data Storage & Security

All client data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We are ISO 27001:2022 certified and SOC 2 Type II audited. Production servers are located in EU (Frankfurt) and US (Virginia) AWS regions.

4. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion ("right to be forgotten").
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Opt out of any processing.

Send requests to privacy@technobren.com. We respond within 30 days.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We never sell your data. We share with sub-processors (e.g. AWS, Google Workspace, Slack) strictly to deliver services, under signed Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).

6. Data Retention

Project data: retained for the contract duration + 3 years (legal/audit). Marketing data: until you unsubscribe. Anonymized analytics: 24 months.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

8. Updates

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be notified via email and posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions? Email privacy@technobren.com or write to: TechnoBren Infotech Pvt Ltd, B-116 Vibhuti Khand, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow 226010, UP, India.
