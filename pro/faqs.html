FAQs

Frequently asked questions

12 things clients usually ask before signing. Have a different question? Email us — we reply within an hour.

How long does a typical project take?+
MVPs ship in 4-6 weeks. Mid-size apps 8-16 weeks. Enterprise platforms 4-9 months. Every estimate has a free 30-min discovery call.
How do you price projects?+
Three models: Fixed Price for clear scope, Time & Material for evolving scope, Dedicated Team for long-term. Hourly rates start at $25/hr; monthly dedicated from $2,800/mo.
Do you sign NDAs?+
Yes — we sign your NDA before any commercial discussion. We never sub-contract. All IP is fully assigned to you on payment.
Can I hire a dedicated team?+
Yes. Pick from our pre-vetted bench (or interview & pick). Full-time engineers, your tools, your time zone, our payroll. 48-hour onboarding.
Where are your engineers located?+
India (Lucknow & Jaunpur) for the bulk; Dubai for client-facing PMs; Uganda for regional support. We work in any time zone with 4+ hrs daily overlap.
Do you offer post-launch support?+
Yes — 24/7 SLA-backed support with under-15-minute P0 response. Monthly support packages start at $1,500/mo.
What about data security & compliance?+
SOC 2-aligned controls, ISO 27001 readiness, GDPR/HIPAA/PCI-DSS-ready engineering. We can sign DPAs and BAAs as needed.
Will I own the source code?+
100%. On full payment, all custom-developed code, designs and deliverables are assigned to you. We retain only pre-existing libraries (granted to you under perpetual royalty-free licence).
Can you work with our existing team?+
Absolutely. Most engagements are blended — we plug into your Jira/Linear, Slack/Teams, and stand-ups. We don't need to own everything.
Do you guarantee performance?+
We commit to: Lighthouse 95+ on web, <3s LCP, <100ms API P95, 99.9% uptime SLA. If we miss, we fix at no charge during 90-day warranty.
What if I need to scale up the team mid-project?+
We can add resources within 1-2 weeks. Either from our bench, or fast-tracked recruits. Min commitment is 1 month.
Do you do project management or just dev?+
Both. Every engagement gets a senior PM at no extra charge for projects >$25K. They run sprints, demos, status reports and risk tracking.
