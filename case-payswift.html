The Challenge

PaySwift's existing gateway timed out under load and ran on a single Mumbai region. They needed a globally redundant, PCI-DSS Level 1 white-label gateway resellable to banks and PSPs.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the core into a multi-region, smart-routing gateway:

  • Smart routing — picks optimal acquirer per transaction (success-rate × fee).
  • Multi-region active-active — Mumbai, Singapore, Frankfurt, Virginia.
  • Tokenisation vault — PCI-DSS L1, network tokens, 3DS2 step-up only when needed.
  • Reliability engineering — circuit breakers, idempotency keys, exactly-once settlement.
  • Reseller portal — banks rebrand, set fees, manage merchants in white-label.
