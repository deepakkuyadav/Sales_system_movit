The Challenge

Average ER wait was 4h 12min. Manual triage by overworked nurses led to 12% under-triage of urgent cases. The client wanted an AI co-pilot — not a replacement — that nurses would actually trust and use.

  • Patient-data sovereignty across 7 countries
  • Sub-second inference at the bedside on tablets
  • Explainable decisions auditable by regulators
  • Integration with 4 different EHR vendors

Our Approach

We built a clinical-grade triage co-pilot with humans always in the loop:

  • Custom ensemble model — gradient boosting + transformer trained on 2.1M anonymised triage records.
  • FHIR-native integration — connects to Epic, Cerner, Meditech & Dedalus via FHIR R4.
  • Explainability layer — every score shows top-5 contributing factors and confidence interval.
  • On-prem deployment — fully air-gapped option for data-sovereignty-strict regions.
  • Continuous evaluation — weekly drift reports reviewed by the clinical board.
