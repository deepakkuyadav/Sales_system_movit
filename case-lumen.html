The Challenge

Lumen wanted to bring 1-on-1 tutoring economics to mass-market K-12 students. The challenge: simulate a great human tutor cheaply enough to charge $9/month — and prove improvement on standardised exams.

Our Approach

We combined an adaptive learning engine with a domain-tuned LLM tutor:

  • Knowledge graphs — every concept mapped to prerequisites, mastery probabilistically inferred (BKT).
  • LLM tutor with guardrails — Claude-based, refuses off-topic, never reveals answers, Socratic-style.
  • Per-student pacing — spaced repetition, adaptive difficulty, weekly mastery reports for parents.
  • Mobile-first — Flutter app, offline mode, voice-input for younger students.
  • Outcome study — RCT in 12 schools showed 38% improvement on year-end exams.
