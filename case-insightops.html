The Challenge

InsightOps wanted to take on Datadog and Grafana Cloud at the SMB-to-mid-market segment with a "honest pricing" angle. They had a working MVP but couldn't scale ingestion past 50TB/month — and pricing felt as opaque as the incumbents.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the ingestion pipeline and created the industry's clearest pricing experience:

  • Tiered storage — hot (ClickHouse) → warm (S3+Parquet) → cold (Glacier). 70% cost reduction at scale.
  • Multi-tenant by design — Kubernetes per-tenant namespaces with hard quotas.
  • Live cost simulator — customers see exact bill impact before any change.
  • Open telemetry first — zero-vendor-lock-in promise, OTLP-native.
  • Self-serve onboarding — sign-up to first dashboard in < 7 minutes.
