FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with us. Can't find an answer? Ask us directly →

What does TechnoBren do?

We are a global software development company building web apps, mobile apps, AI/ML solutions, ERP/CRM systems, eCommerce platforms and cloud infrastructure for startups, SMEs and enterprises across 35+ countries.

Where are you based?

We have 4 offices: Lucknow (India HQ), Jaunpur (India), Dubai (UAE) and Uganda (US/Africa operations). 60% of our team works remotely-first.

How big is your team?

200+ engineers, designers, project managers and strategists. Average engineering experience: 6.4 years.

How much does a project cost?

Fixed-price from $2,499. Dedicated team from $18/hour/dev. T&M from $25/hour. Try our interactive Project Estimator for an instant ballpark.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes — 10% for non-profits, 15% for early-stage startups (pre-seed), and volume discounts on multi-project commitments. Annual contracts get up to 20% off.

What payment methods do you accept?

Wire transfer (USD/EUR/GBP/AED/INR), Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay and crypto (USDC/USDT). Enterprise clients can use NET-30 terms.

Are there any hidden costs?

Never. Every quote is itemized and fixed. Third-party costs (cloud hosting, paid APIs, app-store fees) are transparent and pass-through at cost.

How long does a typical project take?

MVPs ship in 4–8 weeks. Mid-size projects 3–4 months. Complex platforms (ERP/AI) 4–6 months. We work in 2-week agile sprints with weekly demos.

What does your delivery process look like?

Our 6-step framework: Discovery → Design → Develop → QA → Deploy → Support. See full methodology →

How do you handle scope changes?

Change Requests are documented, estimated, and approved before any work — no surprises. Most clients budget a 10–15% buffer for change requests in fixed-price contracts.

What time zones do you work in?

4-hour overlap with any client time zone, guaranteed. Critical-incident support is 24/7 via dedicated Slack/Teams.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — mutual NDAs at first contact. We can sign your custom NDA or provide ours.

Who owns the source code?

You do. 100% IP transfer is included in every contract. Code is delivered via your private GitHub/GitLab with full commit history.

What about data security?

ISO 27001:2022 certified, SOC 2 Type II audited. All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). GDPR / HIPAA / PCI-DSS compliant on demand.

Do you provide a warranty?

60-day post-launch bug-fix warranty on all releases. Plus we offer paid support & maintenance plans starting at $499/month.

How quickly can I hire developers?

Bench developers within 48 hours, custom-matched team within 5–7 days. 7-day risk-free trial included on dedicated team engagements.

Can I interview developers before hiring?

Absolutely. We provide CVs + 30-min technical interviews + paid 1-week trial sprint. You choose who joins your team.

What if a developer doesn't perform?

Free replacement within 7 days, no questions asked. Hours billed during onboarding of replacement are not charged to you.

Can I scale my team up or down?

Yes — 2 weeks' notice for scale-down, 1 week for scale-up. No long-term commitment required.

Still have questions?

Our team typically replies within 1 business hour.
