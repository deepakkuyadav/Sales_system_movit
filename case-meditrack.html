The Challenge

Independent pharmacies in the chain ran 5 different POS systems. Inventory was reconciled monthly, expiry losses were significant, and store managers couldn't see what neighbouring branches had in stock.

Our Approach

We built MediTrack — one platform for all 240 stores:

  • Real-time inventory sync — every sale updates network stock in < 1 second.
  • Demand forecasting — store-level forecasts using 3 years of POS data + local events.
  • Expiry automation — auto-FEFO, near-expiry alerts, inter-store transfers.
  • Prescription validation — drug-interaction checks, schedule-H compliance.
  • Owner dashboard — chain-wide P&L, by-SKU profitability, vendor scorecards.

The Results

↓ 60%Stock-out incidents

We were terrified of forcing 240 store owners onto one system. The migration playbook TechnoBren built — store-by-store, weekend cutovers, hyper-care — made it almost boring.
