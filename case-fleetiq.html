The Challenge

The client's existing TMS gave routes once a day, ignoring traffic, weather and live telematics. Drivers improvised on the road, costing fuel, time and customer SLAs.

  • Sub-30-second route re-plan when conditions change
  • Telematics from 6 different OEMs
  • Driver-friendly mobile UX (no training)
  • Replace a $3M/yr legacy system

Our Approach

We built a streaming route-optimisation engine with a driver-first mobile app:

  • Real-time OR-tools solver — re-optimises every 90 seconds with traffic and ETA inputs.
  • Telematics fabric — normalised feed from Geotab, Samsara, Verizon Connect & OEM APIs.
  • Driver app (Android) — turn-by-turn, voice-first, works in dead zones with cached maps.
  • Fuel intelligence — eco-score per driver, gamified leaderboards.
  • Dispatcher console — bird's-eye fleet view, what-if simulations.
