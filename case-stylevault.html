The Challenge

StyleVault wanted to be the Asia-fashion alternative to global marketplaces — curated, fast, with strong seller tools. The legacy Magento marketplace plugin couldn't scale past 1,500 sellers without breaking.

Our Approach

We rebuilt the marketplace from the ground up with sellers and discovery as the priority:

  • Seller Cockpit — listing automation, bulk image AI tagging, AI product copy in 3 languages.
  • Visual search & AI styling — upload an outfit photo, get matching products from 12K sellers.
  • Split payments & escrow — multi-currency payouts to 9 countries, instant for verified sellers.
  • Reverse logistics — automated returns with photo proof, fraud-detection AI.
  • Personalised feed — per-user discover feed, +47% AOV vs old grid.
