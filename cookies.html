Cookie Policy

Small text files that make our website work better — here's exactly what we use.

Last updated: 1 May 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how you interact with the page. Some cookies are set by us (first-party); others by trusted partners (third-party).

Types of cookies we use

We classify cookies into three categories:

  • Essential — required for the site to function. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics — help us understand traffic and improve the site. Optional.
  • Marketing — used to measure ad campaigns and personalise content. Optional.

How to manage cookies

On this site

Use the cookie banner that appears on your first visit, or click "Cookie settings" in our footer at any time to change your preferences. Your consent is recorded in tb_consent.

In your browser

All major browsers let you block, delete or limit cookies:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: blocking essential cookies will break parts of the site (forms, login).

Do Not Track

We honour the browser's "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal — when enabled, we disable analytics and marketing cookies for your session.
